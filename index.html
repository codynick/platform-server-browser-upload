Mix real and simulated parts in one program

Every gadget block can use real hardware or a simulation. Add a dashboard or a sprite when you want to see or control more. The built-in examples cover every combination.

  • Hardware

    Plug in a CodyJoy Pro over USB. Read its sensors, light its RGB LEDs and use its built-in joystick.

  • Simulation

    Add virtual gadgets, including sensors, an LED and a seven-segment display, and change their readings by hand. No board needed.

  • Dashboard

    Open live gauges and graphs beside the editor, and add buttons, toggles and sliders that your program can read.

  • Sprite

    Put a sprite on a stage in its own tab. Pick from eleven appearances, then move it, resize it and make it speak.

How a session goes

  1. 1

    Connect

    Plug your board into your computer and choose its serial port in the editor. Skip this step to use simulation.

  2. 2

    Build

    Snap blocks together or write Python. Choose Hardware or Simulation on each gadget block.

  3. 3

    Run

    Press Run, open the dashboard or stage, and download your project when you want to keep it.

Before you connect a board

  • Use Chrome on a computer or an Android device.
  • Your CJP needs the CodyNick gateway firmware. The editor can check it and upload it for you.
  • Close other programs that use the board's serial port.
  • Programs run in your browser, so keep the editor tab open and connected while they run.
