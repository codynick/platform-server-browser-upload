Run on the Debian/Ubuntu server:

wget -4 -O /tmp/codynick-ai-setup.sh "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codynick/platform-server-browser-upload/AI/setup.sh?$(date +%s)" && sudo bash /tmp/codynick-ai-setup.sh

Voice Q&A: Examples > AI > AI + Dashboard - Talk and Hear an AI Answer.
Run, click Talk, ask one short English question and pause. Spoken answers require a local system voice.

Voice: Show Spaces > AI > Speech Recognition > Mode: Voice Commands > Start Listening. Say red, green or off, then pause.
Example: Examples > AI > AI + Dashboard - Voice Controlled Light.
First speech use loads about 45 MB; allow microphone access. Use Enable Microphone Audio if requested by your browser.

Transcription: AI > Speech Recognition > Mode: Speech to Text > Start Listening. Stop finishes the last phrase; Copy/Download in Results.
Example: Examples > AI > AI + Dashboard - English Speech to Text.

TTS: AI > Text to Speech > enter text > choose a local voice > Speak. If no local voice exists, try another browser/device.
Example: Examples > AI > AI + Dashboard - Local Text to Speech. If blocked, click Enable Speech in AI.

LLM: AI > Text Assistant (LLM) > short question > Generate Answer. First model load is about 370 MB and needs substantial RAM; test a laptop/desktop first.
Example: Examples > AI > AI + Dashboard - Small Local Text Assistant. Stop / Unload releases the model. Verify answers: this small model can be wrong.
